## Voice in one line

Practical, honest, plain-spoken, customer-first — **Discover, Qualify Hard, Tell the Story, Listen and Stay Honest.**

## Document-specific additions

- **Customer first** — open every section with the customer's situation or outcome, not the product
- **Honest about fit** — state assumptions, gaps, and open questions in their own section; never bury them
- **Short sentences** — 15–20 words per sentence in body copy
- **Active voice throughout**; plain words over jargon
- **Section headings** are Title Case; body is sentence case
- **Evidence over adjectives** — a number with its source beats "significant improvement"; mark estimates as estimates
- No clichés: avoid "end-to-end", "best-in-class", "world-class", "seamless", "synergy", "holistic"
- Dates: DD Month YYYY (e.g., 28 September 2026)
- Numbers: numerals — "18% reduction", not "eighteen percent reduction"

## Document closing standards

Proposals: "We look forward to working with you on this."
Letters: "Yours sincerely," (named) / "Yours faithfully," (unnamed)
Summaries: "Happy to go through any of this in detail — and to hear where we got it wrong."
