# Video Demo Creator

Guides the full lifecycle of a presales demo video — brief, script, recording prep, editing, and validation.
Every video follows the **Plan–Create–Check cycle**: Plan (70% of effort) → Create → Check
(not to be confused with demo-storyboard's PCV, Pain-Capability-Value).

**Narration voice:** a recorded video addresses the viewer directly ("you"); live demos built
with demo-storyboard use first-person persona narration instead ("I'm Maria, and every Monday I…").
Don't mix the two in one video.

Full reference: `references/Video_Recording_Best_Practice_Guide_V3.md`

## Step 1 — Select the video level

Choose the right level before anything else. The wrong format for the funnel stage is the most common wasted effort in demo video production.

| Level | Type | Funnel stage | TELL 1 | SHOW | TELL 2 | Total | Use it when… |
|-------|------|--------------|--------|------|--------|-------|---------------|
| **L1** | Teaser / Overview | Top of funnel | 15 sec | 55 sec | 20 sec | 60–90 sec | Sparking interest — one wow moment, no full workflow |
| **L2** | Product Demo On-Demand | Mid funnel | 30 sec | 2–4 min | 30 sec | 3–5 min | One persona, one use case for outreach or a guided buyer story |
| **L3** | Deep Dive Demo | Mid funnel | 45–60 sec | 3.5–8 min | 45–60 sec | 5–10 min | Qualified leads who want end-to-end workflow detail |
| **L4** | Industry Use Case | Evaluation | 45–60 sec | 3.5–8 min | 45–60 sec | 5–10 min | Full end-to-end business story for one industry |
| **L5** | Live Demo / PoC Recording | Bottom of funnel | 3–5 min | 15–35 min | 2–5 min | 20–45 min | Record every live session for coaching and reuse |

**The rule:** if your video runs longer than its level maximum, split it into separate videos.
Every row satisfies checkpoints 4 and 5 (TELL 1 ≤ 20% of runtime, SHOW ≥ 60%); scale TELL 1
with the total when picking values inside a range (a 20-min L5 gets a 3-min TELL 1, not 5).

---

## Step 2 — Fill in the Video Brief

**Spend 70% of your time here — most videos fail because teams skip this step.** Fill in every
field; if you cannot answer one, the video is not ready to script yet.

```
VIDEO BRIEF

Video level:        L1 / L2 / L3 / L4 / L5
Video type:         Teaser / Product Demo / Deep Dive / Industry Use Case / Live Demo
Target persona:     [Role, seniority, industry, company size]
Top pain point:     [What keeps them up at night — 1 sentence, not a feature list]
Industry focus:     [e.g. Manufacturing / Healthcare / Retail / Financial Services / Other: ___]
Product focus:      [Max 1–2 modules or use cases — if more, make two videos]
Hook (first 10 sec):[One question or statement that hits their pain right away]
Max length:         [60–90 sec / 3–5 min / 5–10 min]
Format:             [Avatar (Synthesia) / Human cam / Screen+Voice / Mix]
Demo share:         [Target: min 60% of runtime for demo types]
Key messages:       [Max 3 — if you need more, make two videos]
Call-to-action:     [One only: request demo / book meeting / explore more / watch next video]
Slides needed:      [Max 2–3 for videos under 5 min. No agenda slides. No company overview]
Buyer story:        [Optional — if clips are combined into a guided story: what comes before and after it?]
```

If any field above is blank, fill it yourself from the persona, industry, pain, and product
context already given: draft the hook, up to 3 key messages, one mid-demo objection, and a
value statement, in simple active-voice language that addresses the viewer as "you".

---

## Step 3 — Generate the hook

The first 10 seconds decide if someone watches or scrolls. Write 5 candidate opening lines
(under 20 words each, one per format: contrarian statement, surprising statistic, direct pain
call-out, bold prediction, "what if" question) for this persona and topic, rate each 1–10 for
scroll-stop power, and pick the winner.

If the hooks feel generic, dig deeper first: list the persona's top 5 daily emotional pain
points as internal monologue (what they think but don't say out loud), real and specific, no
corporate language, then turn the sharpest one into the hook.

---

## Step 4 — Write the script

Using the video brief from Step 2, write the complete script as a 4-column table (Time /
Visual-Action / Narration / Direction Note), sounding like you're talking to a colleague.
Follow Tell-Show-Tell strictly (TELL 1 → SHOW → TELL 2), keep demo share at minimum 60% of
total runtime, frame every capability from the persona's view rather than listing features,
keep sentences short and active with "you" language, never open a sentence with "We," and
close with exactly one CTA.

**Script writing rules (apply to every script):**

| Phase | Must include |
|-------|--------------|
| TELL 1 — Set the stage | Persona introduced within 15 sec; their challenge stated, not your product; max 1–2 slides (no agenda, no company intro); emotional hook in the first 10 sec |
| SHOW — Demo in action | Fullscreen only, no split screen; real user flow from the persona's view; narrate what the persona achieves, not which buttons they click; mouse moves slowly, cursor highlight on; min 60% of total runtime; handles 1–2 objections mid-demo (L3+) |
| TELL 2 — Value and close | Concrete outcomes — time saved, errors reduced, risk removed; one final slide maximum; one CTA only, spoken and on-screen match |

---

## Step 5 — Pre-recording checklist

Run this before hitting record. Problems found here take 5 minutes to fix. Problems found after recording take 3 hours.

| Category | Checks |
|----------|--------|
| Environment | Demo sandbox/tenant only, never live production data; full workflow rehearsed twice; unnecessary apps closed; OS-level notifications disabled; screen resolution 1920×1080; cursor highlight enabled |
| Audio | External mic set as default input; quiet room, door closed; levels tested — no peaks, no hum |
| Camera (human-cam segments) | Eye level; key light 3–5 ft at 45°, not backlit; real, professional background; dressed professionally |

**Tool decision by video level** (examples only; use whatever your team licenses):

| Level | TELL 1 | SHOW | TELL 2 | Assembly / sharing |
|-------|--------|------|--------|--------------------|
| L1 Teaser | Avatar (e.g. Synthesia) | Screen recording (e.g. Descript) | Avatar | Editor + sharing page |
| L2 Product Demo | Avatar or cam | Screen recording | Avatar or cam | Editor + guided story (e.g. Consensus/goConsensus, Loom) or a shared folder |
| L3 Deep Dive | Cam or slides | Screen recording | Cam or slides | Editor + guided story or shared folder |
| L4 Industry Use Case | Avatar + slides | Screen recording | Avatar + slides | Editor + guided story or shared folder |
| L5 Live Demo | Live (Teams/Zoom) | Live screen | Live | Internal library |

---

## Step 6 — Editing

Edit script-first: cut from the transcript, not the timeline, and remove filler words and gaps over 0.3 seconds.
Add captions or subtitles — essential for silent autoplay on LinkedIn and other feeds.
Export MP4 (H.264, minimum 1080p, 16:9) plus an SRT caption file; any script-based editor (e.g. Descript) covers all three steps.

---

## Step 7 — Script quality audit (before or after recording)

Run this before recording to catch problems early, and again after a first edit to tighten the
final cut. As a senior direct-response copywriter would, rewrite any sentence that starts with
"We," lists a feature without a business benefit, or exceeds 30 spoken words in a 10-second
window, and score the result out of 10 with a one-line rationale. Then traffic-light it against
structure (persona addressed by 15 sec, Tell-Show-Tell boundaries marked, feature-without-benefit
mentions flagged, one clear CTA, demo-share %) and language (persona-fit vocabulary, active
voice, conversational tone, no vague phrases like "many features") — GREEN/AMBER/RED per
criterion, with concrete fixes.

---

## Step 8 — 13-point validation checklist

Run every video through this before publishing. Do not skip it.

| # | Checkpoint | Status |
|---|------------|--------|
| 1 | Video level matches the funnel stage and content plan | OK / Fix |
| 2 | Persona hook lands within the first 10 seconds — no long intro | OK / Fix |
| 3 | Length stays under the defined maximum for this level | OK / Fix |
| 4 | TELL 1 is max 20% of total runtime | OK / Fix |
| 5 | Demo share is min 60% of total runtime (for demo types) | OK / Fix |
| 6 | Demo is fullscreen — no split screen, no distracting toolbars | OK / Fix |
| 7 | No customer data, PII, or live production data visible on screen | OK / Fix |
| 8 | Filler words and pauses longer than 0.3 seconds removed | OK / Fix |
| 9 | Captions / subtitles present | OK / Fix |
| 10 | Audio level consistent — no peaks, no background noise | OK / Fix |
| 11 | Spoken CTA and on-screen CTA match exactly | OK / Fix |
| 12 | Branding: logo, colors, font match your company's brand guidelines | OK / Fix |
| 13 | Sign-off obtained from product expert and sales lead | OK / Fix |

Any Fix → resolve before publishing. A video with open Fix items is not ready.

---

## Step 9 — Publishing and sharing

Individual videos become powerful when combined into a guided buyer experience, using whatever
your team has (e.g. Consensus/goConsensus, Loom, or a shared folder with a short index).
Position videos by buyer-journey stage first (e.g. Problem → Evaluate → Roll out → Run →
Measure, or the customer's own process steps), then by persona, then by use case: a VP
Operations watches the strategic overview while a Process Manager drills into detail — same
product, different stories, one shared place.

After publishing: place the video at the right point in the story or folder index, enable
tracking if the tool supports it, and share the watch-data insights (which videos each
prospect watched, watch time, and which topics sparked interest) with the AE before the next
call.

## Click-through (interactive) demo

A click-through demo is a simulated copy of your product that the buyer navigates at their own
pace, like a test drive (handbook 12.2) — not a video, since the buyer clicks and explores
what matters to them. Build it from the same brief (Step 2).

**Video or click-through?**

| Use a video when… | Use a click-through when… |
|-------------------|---------------------------|
| The story and the narrator matter most (persona, hook, one CTA) | The buyer wants to try it before or between meetings |
| You want a fixed, timed message for many viewers | Several stakeholders each care about a different feature |
| Early awareness or a recap of a live session | Before first discovery, so the live meeting can go deeper (12.1) |

**Structure (keep it short: one use case, 8–15 steps):**
1. Welcome step: persona and the pain in one sentence (TELL 1)
2. Guided path: one hotspot per step on the real workflow, a one-line caption each (SHOW)
3. Value step: the outcome, confidence-tagged, and one CTA (TELL 2)
4. Optional side branches for the 2–3 features stakeholders ask about most

**Tools (examples only):** e.g. Walnut, Navattic, Storylane or Arcade to build it, shared
through a hub like Paperflite or Consensus so every video, click-through and case study sits
behind one link (11.6); use the tool's analytics the same way as video tracking in Step 9.
Use sanitised demo data only — never real customer data.

---

## Quick reference

1. Persona FIRST, never the product. Mistake: the wrong video level for the funnel stage.
2. Hook within the first 10 seconds. Mistake: a long intro jingle or logo animation before the hook.
3. Tell-Show-Tell always, at every level. Mistake: a feature tour with no business benefit.
4. Demo fullscreen, minimum 60% of runtime (for demo types). Mistake: a slide wall instead of a live demo.
5. ONE CTA only. Mistake: multiple CTAs at the end.

Follow Steps 1–9 in order. A simple L2 video (3–5 min) takes 2–3 days; a deep dive, one week max.

- [ ] Hook lands in the first 10 seconds
- [ ] One CTA only — spoken and on-screen versions match
