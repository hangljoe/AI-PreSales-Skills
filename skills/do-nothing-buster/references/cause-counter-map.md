# Status-quo causes → counter-strategies

Source: The PreSales Handbook, 20.1 *The Silent Competitor: Doing Nothing*. The handbook lists nine
causes of inertia, each with a named counter-strategy, plus one cross-cutting counter: emphasise the
costs of inaction. The "Signals" and "Diagnostic question" columns and the "Kit tool" column are this
kit's additions, not handbook text.

Authority: counters marked **(AE)** are commercial levers — payment terms, discounts, guarantees,
refunds. The SC proposes them to the AE; the SC never offers them to the customer.

| # | Cause (20.1) | Signals in deal notes | Diagnostic question | Handbook counters | Kit tool |
|---|--------------|----------------------|---------------------|-------------------|----------|
| 1 | **Fear of change** — new software means effort, training, disruption; familiar workflows feel safer | "Our team is stretched", "bad timing", worry about training or go-live disruption, users absent from evaluation | "What would the first three months look like for your team if you switched?" | Assured onboarding (comprehensive onboarding and training); show ease of use (a learning curve exists, but the design is user-friendly) | `demo-storyboard` (day-in-the-life demo), `/presales:handover:doc` preview of the onboarding plan |
| 2 | **Financial constraints** — upfront cost deters even with long-term ROI | "No budget this year", "freeze", finance not engaged, ROI never validated by finance | "If the numbers work, where would the money come from, and when?" | Flexible payment plans (tiered pricing, monthly vs annual, early-bird) **(AE)**; ROI demonstrations (calculations or case studies over time) | `/presales:value:roi-case`, `business-case-stress-tester`, `pricing-positioning` |
| 3 | **Decision paralysis** — too many options; decision-makers freeze and keep the status quo | Long vendor list, evaluation keeps widening, criteria keep changing, "we need more time to compare" | "Which two or three criteria will decide this?" | Simplified choices (streamlined offer, clear comparison charts); personalised consultations that turn "choice" into "fit" | `/presales:discovery:qualify` (decision criteria), `competitive-battlecard` |
| 4 | **Organisational culture** — "we've always done it this way" | Process owners defend the current method, change initiatives failed before, no executive mandate | "What happened the last time you changed a core process?" | Change-management workshops; success stories from similar organisations | `workshop-agenda-builder`, reference customers from your library |
| 5 | **Lack of awareness** — they do not know what is possible; inaction without resistance | Pain accepted as normal, no benchmark, surprise at what the demo shows | "How do peers in your industry handle this today?" | Educational marketing (webinars, content, free trials); presence at industry events | `demo-storyboard` (the art of the possible), `field-comms-writer` (share tailored content) |
| 6 | **Previous negative experiences** — a past bad implementation makes them wary | "We got burned last time", scepticism about claims, heavy demands for proof | "What went wrong last time, and what would have to be different?" | Transparency (clear about what the product can and cannot do, genuine testimonials, open feedback channel); guarantees (satisfaction guarantee, hassle-free refund) **(AE)** | `/presales:deal:poc-plan` (prove it on their data), `confidence-tagger` |
| 7 | **Long-term commitments** — contractually tied to a current product or service | Incumbent contract running, renewal date far away, switching penalties | "When does the current contract end, and what does leaving cost?" | Interoperability (works alongside the tools they already use); migration assistance (support and tools to move over) | `integration-complexity`, a phased plan timed to the contract end |
| 8 | **Stakeholder buy-in** — many people must agree; alignment is the hurdle | New names appear late, EB unseen, champion "needs to socialise it", security or legal not engaged | "Who else needs to say yes, and what does each of them care about?" | Engage stakeholders (present to boards or teams, tailored per group); comprehensive documentation (from security protocols to ROI) | `champion-health`, `/presales:deal:champion-enable`, `exec-briefing-prep` |
| 9 | **Integration issues** — worry about fitting into existing systems | Questions about APIs, data migration, IT scepticism, "our ERP is special" | "Which systems must this work with on day one?" | Robust API and integration options; hands-on integration support | `integration-complexity`, `/presales:handover:osd-draft` |
| ✚ | **Cross-cutting: costs of inaction** | No compelling event, "nice to have", no date attached to the pain | "What does it cost you each month this stays as it is?" | Pain-point identification (show the inefficiencies of today's process); quantify opportunity costs (models of lost revenue or time) | `critical-business-issue-finder`, `/presales:value:roi-case` |

## Scoring scale (this kit's convention)

Score each cause on the evidence in the deal notes, not on gut feel:

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 0 | No sign of this cause |
| 1 | Weak or indirect signal (🟡 Inferred) |
| 2 | Clear signal from behaviour or a second-hand report |
| 3 | Stated by the customer (🟢 Confirmed) or already blocking progress |

Cause scores add up to 0–27. Then adjust for the compelling event:

- A confirmed compelling event with a date: subtract 3 (never below 0).
- No compelling event, or only a vendor-invented one: add 3.

| Total | Status-quo risk | Meaning |
|-------|-----------------|---------|
| 0–5 | Low | Inertia is not the main threat; watch it |
| 6–11 | Medium | One or two causes need a named counter-plan this month |
| 12–17 | High | Treat "do nothing" as the lead competitor in every meeting |
| 18+ | Critical | The deal will likely slip or die without an urgent, EB-level cost-of-inaction case |

Unknowns are not zero. A cause you could not assess counts as 🔴 Unknown, and each one becomes a
discovery question in the battle plan.
