Build the Opportunity Review Call (ORC) agenda for: **$ARGUMENTS**

The ORC (The PreSales Handbook, chapter 9.3) is a recurring internal call across SC, AE,
Product Management, Development, and Professional Services. Many teams run it
as a weekly deal review. Its purpose is
cross-department qualification — qualify in or qualify out — and to align on what each
function needs to do to win the deal. Not every deal needs an ORC: bring the ones where
functional or technical fit needs discussion, or where the last 10% of requirements need
commitments from other departments.

Paste the current deal status, MEDDPICC scores, the OSD (if drafted), and any open questions.
Share the agenda and OSD with attendees a few days in advance.
