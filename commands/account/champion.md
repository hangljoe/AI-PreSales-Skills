Build a quick champion brief for: **$ARGUMENTS**

This is the **early, multi-stakeholder view**: one page that helps a likely champion talk about
your solution with IT, finance, procurement and their peers when you're not in the room.
It is deliberately short. The Economic Buyer sell has its own kit.

**Siblings:**
- `/presales:champion-health`: score the champion first if you haven't. Below the Developing band (under 13/25), build the relationship before handing over any material (handbook ch. 4).
- `/presales:deal:champion-enable`: the full kit for the Economic Buyer meeting (opener, business case, EB questions, forward email, coaching). Run it only after champion-health says the champion is real. It reuses the one-sentence summary, proof points and cadence from this brief rather than repeating them.

Paste: champion name/title, stakeholders they need to convince, confirmed deal context.
If a deal folder exists, read the discovery summary (02_) and pain-to-value map (04_) first.

### The champion's ask

At the end of each internal conversation, the champion asks for a specific next step:
> "Can I set up a call with [your name] and [stakeholder name] on [date] to walk through
> [the question they raised]?"

Never "are you supportive?" — always a concrete meeting, decision or introduction.

---

### Stay-in-touch cadence

| Touch | Frequency | Who | Purpose |
|-------|-----------|-----|---------|
| Check-in call | Weekly during active evaluation | You + champion | Any new stakeholder concerns? |
| Deal status update | After every significant internal meeting | Champion → you | What happened, what's the sentiment |
| Exec escalation | If the Economic Buyer is going cold | AE + champion | Schedule an exec briefing |
| Thank-you | After each piece of help | You | Acknowledge their effort (handbook ch. 4) |
