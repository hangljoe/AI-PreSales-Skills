# Core Conflict Injections and the Future Reality Tree (FRT)

> **Credit.** Study note in our own words. The Theory of Constraints thinking processes, including the Current and Future Reality Trees, originate with Eliyahu M. Goldratt. This tool, as used here, follows the Black Belt in Thinking (BBiT) programme ([blackbeltinthinking.com](https://blackbeltinthinking.com/)). The method remains the work of its authors.

## What it is

The **core conflict** is the deep dilemma at the root of a Current Reality Tree (CRT). It keeps the whole system stable in its bad state. Fixing single branches rarely lasts because the core conflict pulls things back. A **core conflict injection (CCI)** breaks it.

The **FRT** is the CRT transformed by your injections. It shows where the CCI is not enough, and it tests with cause-and-effect logic that the full solution set really works.

## When to use it in PreSales

- A recurring pattern (late technical wins, POCs that stall) survives every tactical fix.
- Before presenting a solution to leadership, to prove it holds together.
- To turn a customer's CRT into the "future state" story of a business case.

## Steps: core conflict injection

- [ ] Take the key connection of the core cloud.
- [ ] Raise far more assumptions than usual: 10 to 30. The obvious ones have been tried already.
- [ ] Reverse them. Pick for impact, not ease. Give ambitious, long-term options a fair look before dropping them.

## Steps: Future Reality Tree

- [ ] Insert the CCI at the connection it breaks.
- [ ] Trace its effects upward through the chains. How far does the change spread?
- [ ] Move up each chain and stop where the existing injections are not enough. That is a gap.
- [ ] Add injections at each gap until the major undesirable effects are resolved or reduced enough.
- [ ] Check for new negatives with NBRs (see `negative-branch-reservations.md`).
- [ ] List all injections. That list is the solution set for the PRT and NCN.

Aim for **sufficient, not perfect**. Halving a defect rate may be a large win; chasing zero is usually wasteful.

## B2B example

Core conflict in a PreSales team: "support every deal the sales team brings" versus "focus SC time on qualified deals". CCI: "SC engagement is triggered by a shared qualification score that sales leadership owns." The FRT shows a gap: small deals still need help. An added injection closes it: "Unqualified deals get a self-serve demo and a recorded overview."
