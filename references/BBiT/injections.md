# Injections: Solving by Challenging Assumptions

> **Credit.** Study note in our own words. The Theory of Constraints thinking processes originate with Eliyahu M. Goldratt. This tool, as used here, follows the Black Belt in Thinking (BBiT) programme ([blackbeltinthinking.com](https://blackbeltinthinking.com/)). The method remains the work of its authors.

## What it is

An injection is a state that, if it were true now, would break the connection causing your problem. It is worded as a state, never as an action ("the evaluation team has access to sample data", not "email IT for data"). The state is the target. Many different actions can reach it, and the first one you try may fail.

You cannot list ideas that would never occur to you. You *can* list what you assume, then reverse it. Most reversals are useless. You only need one that works.

## When to use it in PreSales

- After building a cloud, druid or NBR, to find the way out.
- When a customer says "we can't do that because…" and you need options.
- To find a differentiated angle against an incumbent.

## Steps

- [ ] Pick the connection to break. In a cloud, prefer one of the action→need links on *your own* side, so no one feels tricked. In a druid, pick a weak link that sits early, before the damage builds.
- [ ] Raise assumptions: "If A then B, because…" or "In order to B, I must A, because…". Do not filter. Aim for ten or more.
- [ ] Reverse each assumption into a possible injection. "Not X" is enough; it need not be the opposite extreme.
- [ ] Pick the one that is realistic and would solve the problem.
- [ ] Test: if this were true, does the connection break? For clouds and druids, check the whole tool resolves, not just one link.
- [ ] Only then plan actions (see `prerequisite-analysis.md`, `transition-logic.md`).

## B2B example

Connection: "If we quote list price, the customer will choose the cheaper competitor."
Assumptions: they compare on licence cost only; they see the products as equal; switching costs are ignored; nobody has quantified the cost of the current process.
Chosen injection: "The buying committee compares options on three-year total cost, including internal effort."
Actions later: a TCO workshop with finance, a reference call on implementation effort.

## Watch for

- Injections worded as tasks. Rewrite as the state the task should produce.
- Stopping at the first two ideas. The useful ones often come later in the list.
